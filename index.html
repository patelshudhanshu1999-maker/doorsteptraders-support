Delete your Doorstep Traders account

For the Doorstep Traders app by Doorstep Traders.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@doorsteptraders.in from the email address registered with your account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. Include the registered name and phone number on the account, so we can confirm it is yours.

We confirm by email once the account is closed. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what we keep

DeletedKept, and why
  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Product reviews and ratings you posted
  • Your cart and notifications
  • Your sign-in credentials
  • Records of orders you placed, including invoices, which we are required to retain under Indian tax and accounting law. These are kept for the statutory retention period and are not used to contact you.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Any order still in progress will be completed and delivered before the account is closed. Questions? Write to support@doorsteptraders.in.
